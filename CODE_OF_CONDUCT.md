# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Nila LocalAI welcomes respectful technical collaboration. Contributors should communicate constructively, review ideas on their technical merits, respect privacy, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or abusive behavior.

## Security and privacy

Do not post credentials, personal datasets, private logs, exploit details for unresolved vulnerabilities, or other people's personal information. Security reports belong in the private reporting channel described in SECURITY.md.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions or participation that violates these standards. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

For conduct concerns involving a maintainer, use a private contact channel rather than a public issue.
