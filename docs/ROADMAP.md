# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Secure foundation
Configuration, offline/network policy, stable errors, audit log, first-run personalization, privacy tests.

## Phase 2 — Local intelligence
llama.cpp manager, Qwen3 1.7B Q4_K_M profile, chat, SQLite FTS memory/knowledge, 8 GB resource manager.

## Phase 3 — Local voice
Multilingual Whisper STT, local TTS, wake phrase, push-to-talk and continuous conversation.

## Phase 4 — Tools and agents
Files/search/edit, terminal, computer adapters, permissions, destructive confirmations, agents, routines and recovery.

## Phase 5 — Connected research
Self-hostable SearXNG adapter, safe fetch/crawl, query sanitizer, provenance/trust metadata, prompt-injection-resistant ingestion and persistent offline retrieval.

## Phase 6 — Product experience
Dashboard, autostart, one-command setup/doctor/repair, verified updates, dismissible release announcements and privacy/memory controls.
